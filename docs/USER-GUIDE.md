# Jotter user guide

## Home

Home shows a greeting, one-click covers to start a new notebook, the notebook you were last in, and your shelf of notebooks (search it or sort by name). Every notebook lives in your notebooks folder, `Documents\Jotter` unless you change it at the bottom of Home or in Settings.

- **New notebook**: click a cover. It is created at once as *Book 1*, *Book 2*… Click its name at the top of the window to rename it; the file is renamed too.
- **Open…** opens any `.ntb` file, wherever it is.
- On a notebook card: pin it to the top, make a copy, or delete it (it goes to the Recycle Bin).

## The notebook window

- **Top bar**: on the left Home, show/hide the sidebar, then where you are: the notebook name (click to rename), the section, and the page name (click to edit). In the middle, over the page, the page's type (Paged / Infinite) and paper (Plain, Ruled, Grid, Dots, Kolam). On the right the save dot, zoom, search, export as PDF and Settings. The dot is green when everything is saved, amber while saving, red if something could not be saved.
- **Sidebar**: sections at the top, the pages of the current section below. Right-click a section or page for rename, colour, duplicate, move and delete. Drag pages to reorder them or onto a section to move them.
- **Tool bar**: grouped as Type · Pen, Marker, Shapes · Eraser, Select, then Undo and Redo, then Focus mode and Full screen, with the chosen tool's options (colours, sizes, text styles, insert) beside them. It sits on the right at first; drag its grip (the six dots) to the left, top or bottom edge, or choose the side in **Settings → Tool bar**. The arrow folds the options away.

## Writing

- **Type (T)**: text style, bold, italic, underline, lists, checklists (click the box to tick), then insert a table, an image, a link (Ctrl+click to open it) or a free text box you can drag anywhere.
- **Pen (P)** and **Marker (M)**: pick a colour and size. Hold **Shift** for a straight line. Lines are steadied automatically; pen lines taper at the ends; a pen with pressure draws thicker when you press harder.
- **Eraser (E)**: *Rub out* removes only what you touch; *Whole stroke* removes a stroke with one touch. A pen's eraser end works too.
- **Shapes (S)**: line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse. Shift makes squares, circles and 45° lines.
- **Select (L)**: draw a loop around ink, then drag it, resize it from the corner, recolour, copy, duplicate or delete.
- **[ and ]** make the current tool smaller or larger. **Space + drag** or the middle mouse button moves around the page.
- **Undo / Redo** (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y) cover typing and ink together, in the order you made them.

## Pages

The switches in the middle of the top bar:

- **Paged** gives A4 sheets. Sheets are separated by a visible gap with the sheet number; **Add a sheet** under the last one adds more room. PDF export splits pages exactly at the gaps.
- **Infinite** gives one page that fills the window and grows as you scroll.
- **Plain, Ruled, Grid, Dots, Kolam** set the paper. Paper colour is in Settings.

## Saving, versions and recovery

Notebooks save themselves a moment after every change. Every ten minutes of work an earlier version is kept: **Settings → Version history** lets you restore one (your current version is kept too). If Jotter closes before saving, it offers to restore your work next time.

## Export and print

**Export as PDF** (Ctrl+E) exports the whole notebook, one PDF page per A4 sheet, with the ink as sharp vector lines. If the notebook mixes A4 and infinite pages it asks whether to include the infinite ones (they are trimmed to what is on them and cut into A4 sheets) or export only the A4 pages; print asks the same. With a coloured paper it also asks whether the PDF background should be white or the paper colour. Settings also offers a page image (PNG), Markdown, print and *Save a copy*.

## View

The page fits the window by itself: actual size when there is room, smaller in a narrow window. Zoom with Ctrl + mouse wheel or touchpad pinch (it zooms around the pointer), Ctrl+= / Ctrl+-, or the zoom control in the top bar; Ctrl+0 (or clicking the percentage) fits the page to the window again. **Focus mode** (Ctrl+Shift+F) hides everything but the page and the tools; Esc leaves it. **F11** is full screen. Settings has light, dark or system theme, three interface sizes, and *Draw with the pen only* for touch screens.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| T P M E S L | Type, pen, marker, eraser, shapes, select |
| [ ] | Smaller / larger |
| Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y | Undo, redo |
| Ctrl+C X V D | Copy, cut, paste, duplicate selected ink |
| Ctrl+PgUp, Ctrl+PgDn | Previous / next page |
| Ctrl+Shift+N, Ctrl+T | New page, new section |
| F2, Delete | Rename, delete (in the sidebar) |
| Ctrl+N, Ctrl+O | New notebook, open |
| Ctrl+F | Search |
| Ctrl+S, Ctrl+P, Ctrl+E | Save now, print, export as PDF |
| Ctrl+\\ | Show / hide sidebar |
| Ctrl+H | Home |
| F11, Ctrl+Shift+F | Full screen, focus mode |
